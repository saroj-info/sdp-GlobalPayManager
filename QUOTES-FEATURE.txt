SDP GLOBAL PAY — QUOTES FEATURE
What was added on 26 October 2026 and how it works
=====================================================

STATUS
------
Built and verified (type-check, engine checks, browser checks, and a real-server
check against the database). The schema was pushed on 26 October 2026 to the
database named in .env. Before quoting customers, enter SDP's real fee schedule
in Templates -> System Settings (see section 9).


1. WHAT IT IS
-------------
A quote is the cost of hiring ONE worker in ONE country for a customer:

   gross pay
 + statutory and employer costs (superannuation, payroll tax, workers' comp, ...)
 = total employer cost
 + SDP fees
 = total quote

Every figure is shown in the country's local currency AND in US dollars, on the
input side and in the preview, at the exchange rate recorded on the quote.
Quotes are saved, numbered (Q-2026-0001, Q-2026-0002, ...), listed, duplicated,
printed to PDF and can be marked Draft / Sent / Accepted / Declined.

The fields a quote stores use the same names and values as a contract
(engagement type, Internal Work / Host Client Work, billing mode, rate type,
rate, currency, country), so a quote can later be turned into a contract.
That "convert to contract" button is NOT built yet; the groundwork is.


2. WHO CAN USE IT
-----------------
SDP internal users only (super admin, admin and agent). Business users, workers
and host clients never see the menu item, and the pages and API refuse them.
Editing the default SDP fee schedule needs a super admin or admin.


3. WHERE TO FIND IT
-------------------
- Sidebar -> "Quotes" (first item in the SDP section) -> list of saved quotes.
- "New quote" button -> the editor (/quotes/new). Opening a row -> /quotes/<id>.
- Templates -> System Settings -> "Quote SDP fee defaults" -> the fee schedule.


4. THE EDITOR, LEFT SIDE (what you enter)
-----------------------------------------
Quote details
  Title, Prepared for (free text), Valid until, optional link to a business,
  Status.

Engagement
  Country ............ one of the countries set up in the system (AU, NZ, US,
                       UK, SG, IE, IN, PH, CA, JP). The currency follows it.
  State / province ... for countries with state rules (Australia's eight
                       states, five US states). A new Australian quote starts
                       on New South Wales so state payroll tax is quoted.
  Engagement category  Employment
                       Independent Contractor (Standard Contractor)
                       Independent Contractor (Contractor of Record Service)
  Engagement type .... the same list the Create Contract wizard shows for the
                       country: Permanent, Fixed Term, Casual Employee,
                       Zero-Hours, At-Will, On-Call, Seasonal, Part-Time,
                       Contractor, Gig Worker. The list depends on the
                       country and on the category.
  Where will the worker work?  Internal Work / Host Client Work.
  Does SDP Global Pay act as billing agent?  Yes / No. Only asked for Host
                       Client Work, because SDP's fees differ when SDP bills
                       the host client itself.

Pay and employer costs
  Pay basis .......... Annual salary, Hourly rate or Daily rate.
  Rate ............... in the local currency; the USD equivalent is shown
                       next to it. Hourly / daily rates also take hours (or
                       days) per week and weeks per year, which the quote uses
                       to work out the annual figure.
  Exchange rate ...... "AUD per 1 USD" (for example 1.52). Filled in
                       automatically from the daily rate; type over it to use
                       your own rate; "Use today's rate" puts the daily rate
                       back. The source and date are printed on the quote.
  Annual / monthly gross pay strip, in both currencies.
  "Employer is above the payroll-tax threshold" switch (see section 6).
  Statutory and employer cost items: one row per item with a tick (include
  or not), description, type, rate and the amount per year in both
  currencies. Items can be edited, removed or added.

SDP fees and quote notes
  Fee lines (tick, description, type, rate or USD amount, who pays, amount
  per year in both currencies) and free-text notes that print on the quote.


5. THE EDITOR, RIGHT SIDE (the preview)
---------------------------------------
The preview is the quote as the customer will see it, recalculated on every
keystroke from the same figures as the left side, so the two can never
disagree. It shows the quote header (number, country and state, engagement,
work location, billing, pay basis, exchange rate with source and date, valid
until), then Monthly and Annual columns for:
  Gross pay
  each statutory / employer cost item that applies, with its basis
  Total employer cost (and on-costs as a % of pay)
  each SDP fee line, with its basis and who pays it
  Total SDP fees
  Total quote per year and per month
  Loaded cost per hour / per day (hourly and daily quotes only)
followed by the notes and a one-line disclaimer. The "PDF" button downloads
the same content as an A4 PDF in the house style (also available from the
list without opening the quote).


6. HOW THE NUMBERS ARE WORKED OUT
---------------------------------
Gross pay
  Annual salary as entered; hourly = rate x hours per week x weeks per year;
  daily = rate x days per week x weeks per year. Monthly = annual / 12.

Statutory and employer cost items
  Seeded automatically from the country cost data already used by the
  Resources page calculator, plus the state rules kept under Templates ->
  Jurisdiction Data:
    Employment ................ the employer on-costs for the country
                                (e.g. Australia: superannuation 11.5%,
                                workers' compensation 1.2%, insurance and
                                admin 1%).
    Standard Contractor ....... the contractor on-costs (e.g. platform and
                                admin 1%, insurances 1%, leave / allowance
                                buffer 8%).
    Contractor of Record ...... the contractor on-costs PLUS the employer
                                items that apply when SDP engages the worker
                                (Australia: superannuation).
  State payroll tax (and US state unemployment insurance) is added for ALL
  THREE categories; untick it on a quote where it should not apply.
  Each item is one of: % of pay, % up to a cap, % above a threshold, or a
  flat amount per year.

  Payroll tax and the threshold switch
  Payroll-tax thresholds (e.g. A$1,000,000 in NSW) apply to an employer's
  TOTAL annual wages, not to one worker. Applying them to a single salary
  always gave zero, which is why payroll tax "was not being calculated".
  The switch "Employer is above the payroll-tax threshold" is ON by default:
  "% above threshold" items are then applied to the whole wage (NSW: 5.45% of
  A$120,000 = A$6,540). Switch it off for an employer below the threshold and
  the threshold is subtracted first.

SDP fees
  Two kinds of line: "% of pay" (a percentage of annual gross pay) and
  "Fixed / month" (a USD amount per month, converted to local currency at the
  quote's rate). Each line records who pays it: the business or the host
  client (host client is only offered for Host Client Work). New quotes start
  from the default schedule for the chosen category and billing-agent answer
  (section 9); every line can be changed on the quote.

Exchange rate
  The server fetches the day's USD rates from open.er-api.com (a free public
  service, no key), keeps them for a day and stores each successful fetch. If
  the service is unreachable the last stored rates are used and marked "last
  known"; if nothing was ever stored the editor asks for a manual rate. The
  rate, its source and its date are saved with the quote, so an old quote
  keeps the rate it was prepared with. USD = local amount / rate.

Totals
  Total employer cost = gross pay + statutory items that apply.
  Total SDP fees = fee lines that apply (split into paid-by-business and
  paid-by-host-client).
  Total quote = employer cost + SDP fees, per year and per month.
  All rounding is to 2 decimals (0 for JPY and VND). The server recomputes
  the totals on every save with the same calculation the preview uses.


7. SEEDING AND "RELOAD DEFAULTS"
--------------------------------
Changing the country, state, category or billing-agent answer reloads the
default cost items and fee lines, but only while you have not edited them.
Once a line has been edited, the quote keeps your lines and shows a "Reload
defaults" link instead of overwriting them. The same applies to the exchange
rate once typed by hand ("Use today's rate" goes back to automatic).


8. SAVING, NUMBERS, LIST, STATUS
--------------------------------
- Save stores the quote and gives it the next number for the year
  (Q-<year>-<4 digits>); numbers never repeat, even when two people save at
  the same moment.
- The list shows number, title and client, country and state, engagement,
  gross pay per year and total per year (both currencies), status and last
  update, with search (number, title, client), status and country filters.
  Row menu: Open, Duplicate, Download PDF, Delete.
- Duplicate makes a new Draft with the next number and "(copy)" in the title.
- Status is set by hand: Draft, Sent, Accepted, Declined. A Draft or Sent
  quote whose "valid until" date has passed is shown as Expired.
- Delete removes the quote permanently (confirmation asked).


9. ADMIN: DEFAULT SDP FEE SCHEDULE
----------------------------------
Templates -> System Settings -> "Quote SDP fee defaults" (super admin / admin).
Pick a category tab and set the "SDP acts as billing agent" switch; the rows
shown are the fee lines a new quote of that kind starts with. Add, edit or
remove rows and press "Save all fee defaults" (it saves every combination at
once). "Load suggested placeholders" fills the current combination with
indicative figures taken from the public pricing page (Employer of Record
USD 399 / month, Contractor of Record USD 349 / month, Contractor management
USD 49 / month, plus a 2% "billing agent fee" paid by the host client when
SDP is the billing agent). These are placeholders: replace them with SDP's
real schedule before quoting customers. Until a schedule is saved, new quotes
start with no fee lines and the editor says so.


10. RELATIONSHIP TO CONTRACTS
-----------------------------
A quote stores, with the contract's own names: country, engagement type,
contractor compliance (blank = Employment, No = Standard Contractor,
Yes = Contractor of Record), "is for client" (Host Client Work), billing mode
(SDP Global Pay as Billing Agent / business invoices the host client /
internal), rate type, rate and currency. The engagement-type list itself is
now one shared list used by both the quote editor and the Create Contract
wizard. A later "Create contract from this quote" action can prefill the
wizard from these fields; it is not part of this release.


11. BEFORE FIRST USE
--------------------
1. Schema: done for the database in .env (tables quotes, quote_fee_defaults,
   fx_rate_snapshots; status enum). Any other environment needs
   `npm run db:push` once.
2. Enter the real SDP fee schedule (section 9).
3. Check the state rules under Templates -> Jurisdiction Data: the built-in
   Australian rates and thresholds are examples marked "edit to current".
4. Production must be able to reach https://open.er-api.com for live rates;
   otherwise users enter the rate by hand.


12. KNOWN LIMITS AND ASSUMPTIONS
--------------------------------
- Exchange rates are indicative (daily, free service), not for invoicing.
- Statutory rates are planning figures; payroll-tax grouping, regional rates
  and industry-specific workers' compensation are not modelled.
- Contractor of Record extras are defined for Australia only (superannuation);
  other countries get the contractor on-costs plus state items until SDP
  specifies more.
- Fees are either % of gross pay or a fixed USD amount per month; there is no
  "% of total employer cost" line type.
- Quotes can be prepared for the ten countries set up in the system (the
  Resources calculator covers seventeen).
- No "send quote" email and no country-based restriction between SDP agents.
